## 🛡️ Core Protection Shields

### 1. 🎙️ CallGuard Audio & Voice Forensics
* **Audio Recording Upload**: Upload any call recording (`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`) directly to inspect speech.
* **AI Whisper Speech Transcription**: Converts audio into text using Groq Whisper (`whisper-large-v3`) with vernacular phonetics.
* **Scam Intent Scoring (0–100)**: Detects coercion phrases (*"CBI Crime Branch"*, *"Supreme Court non-bailable arrest warrant"*, *"MDMA drugs in parcel"*, *"Transfer to RBI clearance escrow"*).
* **Forensic Transcripts**: Displays the conversation with scam keywords highlighted in real time via `<mark class="hit">`.
* **One-Tap Sample**: Includes a built-in *"⚡ Sample Scam Call"* button for instantaneous offline/online testing.

### 2. 💳 Fake UPI Payment Receipt Verifier (OCR)
* **Mod APK Detection**: Detects spoofed payment screenshots generated by fake apps like *SpoofPay* or *Fake PhonePe*.
* **12-Digit UTR Checksum**: Flags receipts with missing or invalid 8-digit/10-digit UTR references (official NPCI UPI transactions strictly require 12 digits).
* **Visual Forensics**: Verifies timestamp sanity (status bar time vs transaction time), font alignment, and NPCI handle integrity.

### 3. 📩 Phishing SMS & TRAI DLT Header Inspection
* **DLT Header Validation**: Decodes Indian telecom sender prefixes and suffixes (`-P` Promotional, `-S` Service, `-T` Transactional, `-G` Government).
* **Unregistered Sender Warnings**: Flags standard 10-digit mobile numbers posing as nationalized banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI).
* **Malicious URL Detection**: Dissects disguised shortlinks (`t.ly`, `.xyz`, `sbi-yono-kyc.com`) targeting net banking credentials.

### 4. 🚨 Emergency Lockdown Shield
* **Automatic Alarm**: Sounds a dual-frequency audio siren (660Hz / 880Hz square wave) and hardware vibration pattern upon high threat detection ($\ge 70\%$).
* **Full-Screen Hardware Takeover**: Uses the Android **Accessibility Service** (`LockdownAccessibilityService`) and **System Alert Window** to prevent victims from minimizing the screen while under active scammer duress.
* **Guardian Telegram Dispatch**: Automatically pushes an emergency alert containing the elder's live GPS coordinates, risk score, and call transcript to the guardian's Telegram.
* **Quick 1930 Helpline**: Direct dial button connecting immediately to the National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930).

### 5. 🌐 10 Vernacular Indian Languages

## 📲 How to Install on Android

1. **Download the APK**: Download `BFX_VESPER_SPAM_DETECTION.apk` on your Android device.
2. **Enable Unknown Apps**:
   - Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Security** (or **Apps & Notifications**).
   - Select **Special App Access** $\rightarrow$ **Install Unknown Apps**.
   - Enable permission for your browser or file manager.
3. **Open & Install**: Tap `BFX_VESPER_SPAM_DETECTION.apk` and tap **Install**.
4. **Grant Recommended Permissions**:
   - **Accessibility Service** *(Required for Emergency Lockdown anti-bypass shield)*.
   - **Display Over Other Apps** *(Required to keep safety alerts visible on incoming scam calls)*.
   - **Microphone & Audio** *(Used for speech processing and CallGuard analysis)*.
   - **Phone** *(Allows one-tap emergency calling to your guardian and 1930)*.
